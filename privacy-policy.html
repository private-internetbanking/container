Privacy / information / trust

Your information, handled with care.

This policy explains what SteelBox Container Co. collects, why we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide when you request a quote, book a consultation, submit a form, contact support, place an order, or ask us to coordinate delivery. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company, delivery address, project details, order references, and messages.

We may also receive basic technical information when you use the site, such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited, and referral information. This helps us keep the site secure and improve its performance.

How we use it

01To provide service.

We use your details to answer questions, prepare quotes, process orders, arrange delivery, and provide client support.

02To communicate.

We may send order updates, delivery information, service messages, and replies to requests you make.

03To protect the site.

We use limited technical information to prevent abuse, investigate errors, secure accounts, and maintain reliable operations.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary details with trusted service providers such as carriers, payment processors, hosting providers, form services, and operational partners when they need the information to perform a service for you.

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose collected, to complete a transaction, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, or protect our business. Retention periods vary by the type of information and the relationship involved.

Your choices

You may ask us to provide, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal and operational limits. You may also ask us to stop using your information for optional communications. To make a request, email steelboxservices@gmail.com and include enough detail for us to identify your records.

We may update this policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated review date.
